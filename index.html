Welcome to Ubuntu Home Staffing

We believe that a well-run home and successful event start with dependable people. Ubuntu Home Staffing connects clients with domestic workers, provides practical training, delivers professional cleaning services and offers catering solutions for different occasions.

Our approach is built on professionalism, respect, skills and the Ubuntu spirit: people helping people.

Our Services

Practical solutions for homes, workers and events.
